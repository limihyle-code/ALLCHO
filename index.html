🍽️ Hunger & Malnutrition
Global Hunger Index 2025 ke according India ka score 25.8 hai (Serious category) aur rank 102 out of 123 countries.

• Undernourishment: lagbhag 12% population (≈ 14–17 crore log) ko regular sufficient food energy nahi milta (GHI / FAO estimates).
• Child wasting: 18.7% — duniya mein sabse unchi rates mein se ek (acute malnutrition).
• Child stunting: ~29–33% children height-for-age se peeche (chronic undernutrition).
• NFHS-6 (2023-24) mein stunting thoda kam hua hai, lekin challenge abhi bhi bada hai.

Sources: Global Hunger Index 2025 (Concern Worldwide & Welthungerhilfe), FAO SOFI reports, NFHS-5/6.
Food distribution & mid-day meal style support systems
📉 Poverty — Progress + Remaining Gap
NITI Aayog Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) ke hisaab se:

• 2013-14 se 2022-23 tak ~24.8 crore log multidimensional poverty se bahar aaye.
• MPI headcount ~29% se gir kar ~11.3% (2022-23) ke aas-paas pahuncha.
• World Bank data ke mutabik extreme poverty ($2.15/day) bahut kam ho gaya hai, lekin lower-middle income line par abhi bhi crores log vulnerable hain.
• Poverty concentration: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, Odisha jaise states mein absoulte numbers zyada rehte hain.

Sources: NITI Aayog National MPI Progress Review, World Bank poverty estimates.
📍 Kaunse areas zyada affected?
Child undernutrition (stunting / underweight) ke higher rates aksar in states mein dikhte hain (NFHS data):

Jharkhand, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Odisha — yahan rural pockets, tribal areas aur low-income households mein nutrition gap zyada hai.

Southern states (Kerala, Tamil Nadu) nutrition indicators mein better hain, lekin unmein obesity / diabetes ka dual burden badh raha hai.

Source: NFHS-5 & NFHS-6 state factsheets.
Primary health & maternal-child care — critical for next generation
🏥 Major Health Burdens (Real Data)
Tuberculosis (TB)
India global TB cases ka ~25% share rakhta hai. Incidence ghat raha hai (≈187/lakh 2024), treatment coverage ~92%, lekin drug-resistant TB aur malnutrition se linked cases abhi bhi challenge hain. Absolute cases UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, MP mein zyada.

Child & Maternal Nutrition linked diseases
Anaemia women (15–49) mein bahut high (NFHS data ~50%+ range historically). Pneumonia aur diarrhoeal diseases abhi bhi under-5 mortality ke important causes hain.

Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)
Diabetes, hypertension, heart disease — ab India ke total deaths ka bada hissa (~65% NCDs). Diabetes burden crores mein hai; prediabetes bhi bahut high.

Double burden
Ek taraf undernutrition, doosri taraf overweight/obesity aur lifestyle diseases — ye dual challenge hai.

Sources: WHO Global TB Report, ICMR, NFHS, SOFI / FAO, India disease burden studies.
Together we can reduce suffering — awareness + action
💚 ALLCHO kyun yeh sab dikhata hai?
Kyunki app sirf coaching, hostel, mess, PG search nahi hai. Hum chahte hain ki users ko pata chale ki India mein abhi bhi crores log nutrition, basic health aur dignity ke liye struggle kar rahe hain.

Isliye app ki saari earnings ka 100% donation ke liye commit kiya gaya hai. Har donation detail yahan transparent rahegi — daily report, kitna aaya, kitna diya, kis cause / organisation ko gaya.

Aap Donate button se seedha contribute kar sakte ho. Chhota amount bhi farak laata hai.
Rural livelihoods — many families still depend on uncertain daily wages
👥 Who is most affected?
Children under 5 — stunting & wasting se growth, immunity aur school performance dono affect hote hain. GHI ke according India child wasting mein duniya ke sabse bure rates mein hai.

Pregnant & lactating women — anaemia aur poor diet se mother + baby dono risk mein.

Elderly & daily-wage workers — fixed income nahi, health cost high, nutrition compromise hota hai.

Tribal & remote rural pockets — access to anganwadi, PDS, primary health centre uneven hota hai.

Yeh “abstract number” nahi hain — yeh real families hain jinke paas har din enough, nutritious food nahi pahunchta.
🗺️ State-wise snapshot (higher burden areas)
Bihar & Jharkhand — child undernutrition indicators historically high; large rural population, low income density.
Uttar Pradesh & Madhya Pradesh — absolute numbers bahut bade hain kyunki population bhi badi hai.
Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Odisha — tribal belts + drought/climate stress se nutrition pressure badhta hai.
Urban poor pockets (metro slums) — calorie mil sakti hai lekin protein, iron, diversity kam; anaemia common.

Kerala / TN jaise states nutrition mein better, lekin wahan obesity + diabetes ka dual burden rise kar raha hai.

Source: NFHS state factsheets, NITI Aayog MPI state patterns.
Helping hands matter — mid-day meals, anganwadi, community kitchens
🦠 Disease reality linked to poverty & nutrition
Tuberculosis — India ~1/4 of world TB cases. Malnutrition TB risk ko badhata hai; TB recovery ke liye bhi achhi nutrition zaroori hai. High absolute cases: UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, MP.

Anaemia — women of reproductive age mein prevalence historically 50%+ range (NFHS). Iron deficiency se energy, pregnancy outcomes, child cognition sab affect.

Childhood pneumonia & diarrhoea — ab bhi under-5 deaths ke important causes; clean water, sanitation, nutrition se linked.

Diabetes & hypertension — NCDs ab deaths ka majority (~65%). Urban + rural dono mein rising. “Double burden”: kuch log undernourished, kuch overweight — same country, same system.

Sources: WHO TB Report, NFHS, ICMR, Global Burden of Disease style estimates.
✅ Kya kaam karta hai? (evidence-based)
• POSHAN Abhiyaan / ICDS / Anganwadi — early childhood nutrition & growth monitoring
• Mid-Day Meal — school attendance + calorie support
• PDS (ration) — grain access, lekin diet diversity abhi bhi challenge
• Janani Suraksha / maternal schemes — institutional delivery, anaemia care
• TB Nikshay + nutrition support — treatment + food support combination
• Community kitchens / NGO feeding — local gaps fill karte hain

Data dikhata hai progress ho raha hai (MPI down, extreme poverty down), lekin child wasting / anaemia / TB absolute burden abhi bhi bada hai — isliye continuous support chahiye.
Every child deserves food, health and a chance to learn
📊 Numbers dobara — taaki yaad rahe
• GHI 2025: India 102 / 123, score 25.8 (Serious)
• Undernourishment still in crores (GHI / FAO range ~14–17 Cr order)
• Child wasting 18.7% — among the worst globally
• Stunting still ~3 in 10 children under five (improved but high)
• MPI: ~24.8 crore exited multi-dimensional poverty (2013–23) — progress real hai, journey incomplete hai
• TB: India still largest share of global cases
• Healthy diet unaffordable for a large share of population (FAO SOFI findings)

Ye numbers isliye hain taaki koi bole “sab theek hai” to data saamne ho.
